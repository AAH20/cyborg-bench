# Security and Benchmark Integrity Policy

## Benchmark Integrity
`cyborg-bench` evaluates physical, robotic, and biological AI systems. Benchmark tampering, test-set data contamination, or artificial benchmark overfitting can compromise safety certifications.

## Reporting Vulnerabilities or Benchmark Cheating
If you discover a benchmark leakage vulnerability, metric manipulation vector, or cryptographic signing flaw:
1. Email the maintainers directly at **security@a2zsoc.com** with the subject line `[BENCHMARK-INTEGRITY] cyborg-bench report`.
2. Provide full reproduction code and proof of metric bypass.
3. Vulnerabilities will be addressed within 24 hours.
